cadrs in the browser at full speed, more of who you're working with, and rebuilds that show their progress.

## Downloads

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux x86_64 (glibc 2.35+: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or newer) | `cadrs-v0.2.2-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` |
| Windows x86_64 (Windows 10 or 11) | `cadrs-v0.2.2-x86_64-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel | `cadrs-v0.2.2-universal-macos.dmg` |
| A browser with WebGPU, such as Chrome or Edge | <https://cadrs.net/app/>, or `cadrs-v0.2.2-web.tar.gz` to serve yourself |

The Linux and Windows archives hold the executable, its `libraries/` folder (the built-in symbols, footprints and 3D models), the README and the licenses. Nothing needs installing: unpack it and run `cadrs` (Linux) or `cadrs.exe` (Windows), keeping `libraries/` beside it. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the files' checksums.

On macOS, open the `.dmg` and drag cadrs to Applications. The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS won't open it: right-click cadrs in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (on macOS 15 and later, open it once, then choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security). Updates on macOS are downloaded from the release page and dragged over the old app.

On Linux, the app needs a Vulkan-capable GPU driver, and ALSA and udev (present on desktop distributions). The Windows build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Documents are saved in `~/.local/share/cadrs/` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\cadrs\` on Windows and `~/Library/Application Support/cadrs/` on macOS.

## What's new since v0.2.1

- **Faster, in the browser most of all.** At <https://cadrs.net/app/> a sample document now draws at the screen's full 60 frames a second, where it managed about 19. The desktop app takes about a third less time per frame too.
  - The frame panel (F12) no longer times every system unless you ask, since timing them cost about a fifth of each frame. To see them, turn on **Preferences › Interface › Time systems**; it takes effect the next time cadrs starts.
- **A long rebuild shows its progress** (#168):
  - The feature list marks each feature as done, busy (with a spinner) or queued, and scrolls to keep the busy one in view.
  - The view shows the parts as they are built.
  - An edit made during a rebuild stops it and carries on from the change, rather than waiting for the whole list.
- **More of who you're working with** (#157). In a shared document:
  - The others' avatars sit in the top bar. Click one for a card that shows where they are and what they're doing, with **Follow** and **Go to their view**.
  - Tabs and feature rows show the initials of whoever is in that tab, or editing or selecting that feature.
  - Their pointer's label says what they're doing ("Alex · Sketch 1 · Line"). Their selection is outlined in their colour, and what they're drawing shows as they draw it.
  - Their camera shows as a cone of sight. When their pointer is off your screen, a chip at the edge of the view points to it; click it to go there.
  - **Alt+click** sends everyone a "look here" ping.
  - While you follow someone, a banner says so, with **Stop** (Esc stops too).
  - People fade after 2 minutes idle, and turn grey ("reconnecting…") after 6 seconds without a word from them.
  - People still on v0.2.1 can work with you as before.
- **Turning the view** (#91):
  - A held arrow key turns the view smoothly at a steady speed and stops when you let go. A tap still turns it 15°, Shift 90° and Ctrl 5°.
  - Every view animation (arrows, standard views, Normal to, the view cube) is now one turn about one axis.
  - Blender's numpad: 7, 1 and 3 for Top, Front and Right, with Ctrl for Bottom, Back and Left; 9 turns to the other side; 5 switches between perspective and orthographic; 8, 2, 4 and 6 work as the arrows.
  - A default plane seen from behind shows that side's name: Front reads Back, Right reads Left and Top reads Bottom.
- **Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-** step through the UI scale, from 75% to 200%, and a toast shows the size (#167). Toasts now fade out.
- **Documents page** (#161):
  - A document's right-click menu has **Open document folder**.
  - The Trash has **Empty trash…**, and its selection's menu has **Delete permanently…**.
  - The Delete key moves the selection to the trash. In the Trash, it deletes permanently after asking.
- **Modelling:**
  - Chamfering a hole's rim beside a rounded edge froze cadrs (#155). It now fails with an error on the chamfer instead.
  - Rounding the inner corners of a shelled box with a chamfered wall gives the right part (#156).
  - A chamfer exactly as wide as the wall it cuts now displays correctly (#156).
  - More rounds succeed: across narrow wedges, along walls extruded from splines, and where a round meets a pocket's floor.
- **Onshape import:** a round on edges picked from a Split now picks the edges the split cut, so pi7zoll-gehäuse's Verrundung 4 imports (#149).
