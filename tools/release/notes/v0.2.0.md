## Downloads

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| Linux x86_64 (glibc 2.35+: Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 or newer) | `cadrs-v0.2.0-x86_64-linux.tar.gz` |
| Windows x86_64 (Windows 10 or 11) | `cadrs-v0.2.0-x86_64-windows.zip` |
| macOS 11 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel | `cadrs-v0.2.0-universal-macos.dmg` |

The Linux and Windows archives hold the executable, its `libraries/` folder (the built-in symbols, footprints and 3D models), the README and the licenses. Nothing needs installing: unpack it and run `cadrs` (Linux) or `cadrs.exe` (Windows), keeping `libraries/` beside it. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has the files' checksums.

On macOS, open the `.dmg` and drag cadrs to Applications. The app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so the first time macOS won't open it: right-click cadrs in Applications and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (on macOS 15 and later, open it once, then choose **Open Anyway** in System Settings › Privacy & Security). Updates on macOS are downloaded from the release page and dragged over the old app.

On Linux, the app needs a Vulkan-capable GPU driver, and ALSA and udev (present on desktop distributions). The Windows build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: choose **More info › Run anyway**.

Documents are saved in `~/.local/share/cadrs/` on Linux, `%APPDATA%\cadrs\` on Windows and `~/Library/Application Support/cadrs/` on macOS.

## What's new since v0.1.9-beta

- **A new look** (#138):
  - In a document, every panel floats as glass over the 3D view, with a gap the view shows through: the top bar, the toolbar, the rail, the feature list, the side panels and the tab bar. The gaps work as the view: orbit, zoom and pick there too.
  - A cyan accent, and a warm dark scheme taken from the schematic's.
  - The 3D view's ground grid is drawn like the schematic's: faint lines, and a small cross on every fifth line.
  - Tool icons are coloured by family: extrudes blue, revolve orange, sweeps and lofts purple, fillets and chamfers green, cuts red, patterns magenta.
  - Buttons have no outlines. One corner radius everywhere. Buttons squash when pressed, and panels fly in.
  - The feature list hides with the ‹ at its top right; its icon then waits at the end of the rail.
  - Feature dialogs are a little wider, with larger checkboxes and every checkbox lined up.
- **The documents page:**
  - A command strip along the top: New document, New folder, search, Import, New label, the list and grid views and Type.
  - The list sits in a card.
  - Each row has a ⋯ for its menu. Right-click, or ⋯, then **Properties…**, **History…** or **Used in…** opens it in a dialog.
  - A bell for notifications: a newer release, and what moved in this one.
- **Where things moved:**
  - The **cadrs mark** at the top left is the app menu:
    - in a document: All documents, Rename, Export, Create version, Workspace (Main and the branches), Workspace units and Full rebuild;
    - on the documents page: the documents folder, Labels and Trash;
    - everywhere: Preferences and Keyboard shortcuts.
  - The top bar no longer shows Main or the version and branch counters. A branch's or a version's name still shows beside the document's name.
  - The top right is your **account**: your name, your cadrs ID, and Preferences.
- **Identities.** Keep more than one identity on a computer, each with its own cadrs ID: switch between them, make a new one, or rename yours from the account menu. With the network on, switching restarts cadrs as the other identity. Your existing cadrs ID stays the first identity's.
- **Working together:**
  - Share a document by its link, or with people by their cadrs ID, with Can edit or Can view. The two apps connect to each other directly, with no server in between.
  - Edit a shared document together live: the owner numbers everyone's edits, so everyone sees the same document.
  - Everyone's avatars, where they are, and Follow mode.
  - Comments: threads, tags, mentions and tasks.
  - Versions are shared. Main is the shared workspace; branches are your own.
  - A tab that can't be edited together is locked while someone has it open.
  - Keep editing while the owner is away, and transfer ownership.
  - The ✕ on a person's row in the Share dialog stops sharing with them.
- **Section view** can start on a default plane (Top, Front or Right) without picking one.
- **Security:** the zip library is updated for CVE-2026-115905 (#139).

cadrs is an independent project.
